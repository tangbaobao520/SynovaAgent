3.1 增强已有哨兵（7个）— 采用post-processors机制

增强方式：不侵入哨兵的aggregate.ts。在哨兵manifest.json中增加post-processors字段，指向独立ME compute函数。ME逻辑与原有聚合逻辑模块独立。
示例manifest.json片段：{"post-processors": [{"function": "computeEconomicLeverage", "trigger": "DOL>2.0", "injectField": "economic_interpretation"}]}
