A5.5 Implications for Synova A5 Implications PKB (Professional Knowledge Base) design : Synova needs something between Hermes MemoryStore and OpenClaw Dreaming. An "Organizational PKB" that stores: (a) Enterprise-specific diagnosis patterns discovered during FDE sessions, (b) Sentinel-identified recurring anomalies with their resolution history, (c) Expert reasoning traces for future reference. Use OpenClaw's Dreaming pattern: cron-triggered background analysis of accumulated diagnosis history. Information decay is critical for enterprise diagnosis : organizational states change. A diagnosis pattern from 6 months ago may no longer apply. Implement explicit recency half-life (30 days suggested for organizational data) and max age (90 days). Auto-write confidence threshold : for automatically writing conclusions to the PKB, use a tiered confidence system: above 0.95 -> auto-write; 0.80-0.95 -> write with human-visible annotation; below 0.80 -> suggest but don't write. Agent Nudge for diagnosis continuity : during long FDE sessions (multiple measurement cycles), periodically nudge the orchestrator: "We have run N measurement cycles. Are any earlier conclusions contradicted by newer data?" This prevents diagnosis drift. Skills system for diagnosis workflows : successful diagnosis workflows should be captured as procedural skills. "How to diagnose cash flow issues in a 50-person SaaS company" becomes a reusable skill that new diagnosis sessions can load.
